02 Commute

Commute is my attempt at making Jakarta’s rail network feel like one system. It pulls the four operators (KAI Commuter, MRT Jakarta, LRT Jakarta, and LRT Jabodebek) into a single app for station search, live timetables, lines, and interchange hubs, instead of four separate ones.

Under the hood there’s a line-topology model that untangles trunk and branch routing. It’s an installable PWA with offline data caching, running on Cloudflare Workers and D1 at the edge, with a scraping pipeline that keeps schedules current.

The line data powering the avatar up top comes straight from this project, try clicking it!
